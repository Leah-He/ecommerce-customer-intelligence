# E-Commerce Customer Intelligence & Risk Analytics

**Cross-Platform Data Integration | Customer Segmentation | Retention Risk | Business Intelligence**

An end-to-end business analytics project built around a real-world e-commerce operation, combining transaction data from multiple platforms to better understand customer behavior, product performance, and business risks.

The goal was simple: **turn fragmented business data into insights that people can actually use to make decisions.**

## Project Overview

E-commerce businesses often operate across multiple platforms, making it difficult to get a consistent view of their customers, transactions, and products.

This project explored how to connect those fragmented data sources into a unified analytics framework, with a focus on three questions:

- Who are our most valuable customers, and how do we keep them engaged?
- Which customers and products show early warning signs of risk?
- How can transaction data guide marketing, retention, and product decisions?

## What I Worked On

### 1. Data Integration & Standardization
- Designed a framework for combining transaction data from multiple e-commerce platforms.
- Mapped inconsistent source fields into standardized order, customer, and product structures.
- Developed matching and validation rules for cross-platform customer identification.
- Documented data quality challenges, including missing information, inconsistent product names, and refund records.

### 2. Customer Segmentation & Retention
- Applied RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Organized customers into actionable segments based on engagement and value.
- Explored retention risk through purchase recency, transaction frequency, and customer spending.
- Developed rule-based customer follow-up and retention recommendations.

### 3. Product & Revenue Analytics
- Analyzed product performance using sales, order volume, repeat purchasing, and refund indicators.
- Applied ABC classification to distinguish major revenue contributors from lower-performing products.
- Examined regional sales patterns and product category trends.
- Developed a framework for identifying products that may require further review.

### 4. Interactive Business Intelligence
- Built interactive dashboard views using Power BI and a web-based analytics interface.
- Designed filtering and drill-down workflows across customer, product, and geographic dimensions.
- Connected business metrics with customer-level and product-level exploration.

## Key Analytical Methods

RFM Segmentation · Data Standardization · Entity Matching · ABC Analysis · KPI Design · Customer Retention Analysis · Refund Risk Monitoring · Business Intelligence

## Data Privacy & Project Availability

This repository is a portfolio case study based on a real-world business analytics project.

Because the original work involved confidential business information and customer transaction data, the underlying datasets, customer-level records, and certain internal materials cannot be made publicly available.

The original development environment has also been decommissioned, and some historical scripts and intermediate files are no longer available.

Instead, this repository focuses on selected technical documentation, system architecture, analytical methods, and privacy-safe dashboard visuals.

The project is presented to demonstrate the analytical approach, technical design, and business applications rather than as a fully reproducible application.
